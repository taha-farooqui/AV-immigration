Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers about what we do, how long it takes, and what it costs.

What does AlphaVenture do for immigration law firms?

We're the implementation team for 1-25 attorney US immigration firms running on Docketwise, MyCase, Clio, or Camplegal. We configure the platform, build AI workflows around USCIS / EOIR / ECAS notice handling, set up project management and client communications, and stay on as a fractional tech team. Pricing starts at $2,500/mo with a 30-day cancellation; small firms (1-5 attorneys) can engage sprint-only.

Who configures Docketwise for small immigration firms?

AlphaVenture. We built ClerqLegal, the immigration AI that sits on top of Docketwise, so we know the platform's hooks and limits cold. We configure Docketwise for case management, form automation, deadline tracking, and client portals. Typical implementation runs 2-4 weeks.

How long does a Clio or MyCase implementation take for an immigration practice?

2-4 weeks for the implementation sprint. Most firms see their first automations live within the first two weeks; full sprint deliverables land by week four. Retainer clients see continuous improvement month over month.

What does it cost to configure our case-management platform for immigration?

The audit is free. The sprint is fixed-priced and scoped during the audit. Ongoing support starts at $2,500/mo, scoped to firm size and case volume. Solo and small practices (1-5 attorneys) typically engage sprint-only or project-by-project.

We already have someone managing our case-management platform. Why would we need you?

Your admin keeps the lights on. We build the automation layer. If your team is still manually entering USCIS notice data, copying deadlines into calendars, or fielding client status calls, there's a workflow tier your admin hasn't had the time or specialized immigration-tech knowledge to build. We build it, document it, and maintain it, then train your admin to run alongside us.

Do we have to switch case management systems?

No. Most firms already have a core platform; our first goal is to make your current stack work better. If a migration is truly necessary, we'll say so. But we don't start by assuming you need to rip out what you already use.

What if it doesn't work for our firm?

The audit is free, so you see exactly what we'd build before committing budget. The sprint has fixed, agreed scope. If our work doesn't meet the agreed specification, we fix it at no additional cost during the sprint. Retainer clients can cancel with 30 days' notice, no penalty.

Is our firm the right size for this?

We work with 1-25 attorney immigration firms as our primary fit. Solo and small practices (1-5) typically engage us sprint-only or project-by-project rather than on monthly retainer. Mid-size firms (5-25) are the sweet spot for the full audit → sprint → retainer model. From 25-50, we scale the engagement to a full dedicated team.
